BI-FAST bridge over SNAP

A .NET 10 worker service that sits between Octopus and a partner bank's BI-FAST API. It takes ISO 8583 messages from the switch, builds and signs SNAP requests (RSA-SHA256 for the access token, HMAC-SHA512 per request), and maps the bank's answer back to ISO 8583. The switch side is legacy and frozen, so the bridge adapts to it, never the other way round. Secrets, tokens, signatures and card numbers are scrubbed from every log line.

.NET 10ISO 8583SNAP BIBI-FAST
